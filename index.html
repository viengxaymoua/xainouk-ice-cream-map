🗺️ Daim map customer
Nias 📍 Qhia kuv qhov chaw kom pom koj lub cim xiav; nias tus cim customer kom pom cov ntaub ntawv

Nrhiav customer: sau tus lej nyob ntawm hauv ntej lub npe rau hauv lub thawv 🔍 saum daim map.

Daim map xav tau internet. Tso cai Location rau Chrome. Thaum koj mus txog customer, nias “📍 Siv kuv GPS” kom khaws qhov chaw koj sawv; yog xav kho pin kom raug, zoom daim map thiab nias “Xaiv ntawm map”. Cov customer khaws hauv browser ntawm lub xov tooj no.

➕ Ntxiv customer

Tseem tsis tau xaiv qhov chaw. Mus txog ntawm customer ces nias “Siv kuv GPS”, lossis xaiv qhov chaw pin ntawm daim map.

🚚 Txoj kev tag kis / hnub no

Xaiv cov customer ntsuab uas koj xav mus. Hloov qhov kev mus nce lossis nqis raws li koj xav.

    Thaum nias “Tiav kev”, cov customer hauv txoj kev yuav cim hnub no ua hnub xa khoom thiab khaws keeb kwm kev mus.

    👥 Cov customer

    📅 Keeb kwm kev mus xa khoom (30 hnub)

    Xaiv hnub tim kom saib cov customer uas koj cim tias tau mus xa lawm.
    ℹ️ Lus qhia tseem ceeb

    • Hloov hom map ntawm lub pob xaiv saum daim map. “Kev / tsev” qhia kev thiab cov npe chaw; “Satellite” qhia duab saum ntuj thiab koj xaiv ntxiv “Npe chaw saum Satellite” tau.
    • Tus lej saum tus cim yog hnub dhau txij hnub kawg xa khoom. Hnub xa khoom yog hnub 0; hnub tom qab yog 1, thiab ntxiv mus.
    • Thaum dhau 1 hnub txij hnub kawg xa khoom, customer yuav hloov xim liab automatic. Koj tseem hloov xim tau manually.
    • Cov ntaub ntawv khaws hauv browser ntawm lub xov tooj no xwb. Tsis txhob lwv Chrome cov ntaub ntawv yog xav khaws customer.
    • Nrhiav chaw saum daim map siv internet. Suab qhia kev siv GPS, internet, thiab lub suab Thai (yog xov tooj muaj). Suab qhia kev yog kev pab ntxiv; xyuas kev thiab tsheb tiag tiag txhua lub sijhawm.
